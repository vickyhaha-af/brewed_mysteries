The Stage Is Always Open

Open Mic

The microphone is there. The stage is there. Every Saturday and Sunday, the café opens its floor to anyone who feels the call — walk up and perform.

Musicians, poets, storytellers, comedians — no booking, no audition. Order your food, feel the moment, and if it calls you, the stage is yours. Brewed Mysteries is that kind of place. Each gathering also features special guest performers and select invitees who bring something unexpected to the evening.

The Origin

Our Story

✦

Built From
Wanderlust

Brewed Mysteries was born from a single obsession: what if you could journey through the East in one evening? Five nations. Five flavours. One address in Pune where the curious come to eat, drink, and drift.

Every corner was designed in consultation with cultural advisors and chefs from across East Asia, with a deep obsession for detail — from the ceramic ware in Sakura Lane to the lantern glow of the Lantern Quarter.

We are not fusion. We are proximity — each culture held in its truest form, side by side, so you can move between them as you please.
